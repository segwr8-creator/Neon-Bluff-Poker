Play your way

Table style applies when you start a new solo game or create an online room. It does not change a hand already in progress. Casual bots bluff less; Sharp and Chaos bots bluff more. With Neon twists, Chaos excludes Classic. Classic-only rules use normal rankings in every style.

Standard Play

Stack Dollars only. Game units, never redeemable money. No real-money settlements, prizes or resale.

Table stakes. Bet only your starting table stack. No top-ups or withdrawals during a hand. Buy-ins cannot exceed your bank or table limits. Tournament chips (T) remain separate.

Check, call, raise, fold. Check only when nothing is owed. Call pays the difference between your contribution and the current bet; short stacks call all-in. Raises follow the selected variant’s limit. Side pots and ties are settled separately.

Shared information. There is no private card-reveal feature. Showdown cards are sent to everyone at the table.

Timers and disconnects. Betting decisions last 15 seconds. Expiry folds your hand, including when a check was available. A disconnect does not refund committed bets. Draw timeouts stand pat, discard timeouts discard the last card, and Chinese layouts are completed automatically. Settings do not pause a turn.

Live play. At least two humans; no automatic bots. The host deals the first hand, and subsequent hands start after three seconds while the room is active. C: check/call, F: fold, R: raise slider, when shortcuts are enabled.

Game Variants

The menu description explains each game’s deal, draw timing and ranking. Fast-Fold and Jackpot are Hold’em formats. Jackpot multipliers affect tournament scores only.

Neon Bluff only: three private plus two shared cards form exactly five cards. Classic uses normal rankings; Lowball reverses categories and tiebreaks; Red Rush adds one category for three or more red cards; Suit Signal ranks a five-card flush above four of a kind. These twists never change standard Hold’em or Omaha.

7–2 bonuses, straddles and rabbit hunting are not enabled at this table.

Player records & fair play

Your live wallet and recorded decisions are stored on the server. This browser keeps the private credential that restores your profile; clearing it loses access. There is no verified identity, account recovery or real-money value. Display names are not verified. Computer opponents are always marked Computer.

Odds are estimates against random unseen opponent cards, including the current twist. They do not model opponents’ strategies or predict the next shuffle. Nobody receives hidden opponents’ cards.
